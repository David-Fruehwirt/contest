                                                                                                                                                             LEVEL 3
                                                                                                                                                                LEVEL 3Now it’s your turn to run the factory.


▪  You are given a list of molecules that can be bought
▪  You are given a list of possible reactions
      −  Some molecules can only be bought
      −  Some molecules can only be produced by reactions
      −  Some molecules can be both bought and produced

▪  You are given a list of target molecules and quantities, for each:
      −  Start with an empty inventory
      −  Produce at least the given quantity of the target molecule
      −  Reactions can be performed any amount of times
      −  Reactions must only use molecules that are in your inventory or can be bought

This level is an optimization level.
▪  Your score is the total cost of the bought molecules
▪  A lower score is better
